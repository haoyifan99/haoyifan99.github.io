03Research experience

Full background ↗
May – Aug 2026

Applied Scientist Intern · Amazon Rufus

Hosts: Zoey Li and Zhenyu Shi

  • Developed a Muon recipe for RLVR with no momentum, achieving 29.17% macro-averaged Pass@1 on Qwen3-4B-Base compared with 24.47% for AdamW across three math benchmarks.
  • Used spectral diagnostics and checkpoint interventions to study optimization stability and how learned behavior is organized across singular directions and attention projections.
  • Investigated RLVR trajectory predictability with random-walk controls. Read the research write-up ↗
Sep 2024 – present

Ph.D. Student · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Computer Science · Advisor: Prof. Tong Zhang

  • Developed Adaptive Layerwise Perturbation (ALP) to stabilize off-policy LLM reinforcement learning under policy staleness and training–inference mismatch.
  • Proposed DIPNeT for generalization across reasoning and adversarial robustness settings.
  • Developed GVM-RAFT, a prompt-specific sample allocation strategy, achieving a 2–4× speedup and improved accuracy over vanilla RAFT.
  • Studied feature decoupling in Transformers and their expressiveness for hidden Markov processes, and explained how ensemble methods reduce overadaptation in supervised fine-tuning.
